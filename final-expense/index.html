What Seniors Should Know About Covering Funeral Costs

Many families are left paying for a funeral out of pocket. A final expense policy is one simple way to plan ahead.

Most people don't like thinking about it, but funerals are expensive, and the bill usually lands on a spouse or the children within days.

According to the National Funeral Directors Association, the median cost of a funeral with burial is close to $8,000, and that's before the cemetery plot, headstone, flowers and other costs that often push the total past $10,000.

  • Funeral service and burial: about $8,000
  • Cemetery plot and opening/closing: $1,500 to $4,000+
  • Headstone or marker: $1,000 to $3,000

What is final expense insurance?

Final expense insurance is a small whole life insurance policy, usually $5,000 to $25,000, designed to cover funeral, burial and end-of-life bills. The benefit is paid directly to the person you choose, and they can use it however they need.

Who it's for

These policies are commonly available to adults roughly 50 to 85. Many plans need no medical exam, just a few health questions over the phone. Rates are typically locked in for life and coverage doesn't expire as long as premiums are paid.

Pricing depends on your age, health, state and the amount of coverage. A licensed agent can compare options and tell you what's available to you.

Common questions

Do I need a medical exam?
Many final expense policies don't require one. Eligibility and pricing depend on your answers to a few health questions.
Can I buy a policy for a parent?
In most cases the insured person needs to take part in the call and agree to the policy.
Is this a government program?
No. Final expense insurance is offered by private insurance companies.
